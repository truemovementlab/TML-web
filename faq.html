FAQs

Quick answers to the common questions. If you’re unsure, submit the questionnaire and we’ll sort it out together.

1) Who is True Movement Lab coaching for?

This coaching is for people who love to explore the world through movement — any age, any skill level. We typically work with parkour, bouldering/rock climbing, hiking, mountain biking, trail/ultra running, and obstacle course racing. If your sport isn’t listed, that doesn’t mean we can’t help — strong training principles and commitment to progress apply to almost any activity.

2) What sports do you specialise in?

Parkour, bouldering/rock climbing, hiking, mountain biking, trail/ultra running, and obstacle course racing. That said, our approach is flexible — the protocols adapt to the sport. If you move your body in the real world, we can build a plan that fits.

3) Do I need a gym, or can I train with minimal equipment?

Having access to a gym or some basic equipment is strongly recommended — but it’s not required. We can work around your circumstances and build a plan using what you’ve got.

4) How do check-ins work?

Each week, a coach will touch base and communicate with you about your program, progress, and any questions you have. We’re big on keeping you on track through all of life’s ebbs and flows — training should fit your real life, not fight it.

5) What’s included: Program Only vs Coaching?

Program Only includes a tailored program built for your goals, plus weekly text check-ins to discuss progress and adjustments.

Coaching includes everything in Program Only, plus 2–3 check-ins per week with deeper support — including a video call weekly or fortnightly, technique pointers (where relevant), and guidance around recovery + fatigue management.

6) Can you program around injuries or pain?

Yes — and it’s a big part of what we do. We’ll look at history, triggers, and what loads it, then build training that keeps you progressing without constantly poking the bear. That usually means smart exercise selection, range management, gradual re-loading, and building tissue capacity over time. If something needs a medical opinion, we’ll recommend the right professional and program around that guidance.

7) Can you build me a plan for an event / trip / competition?

100%. Reach out as soon as you can and we’ll build a plan that fits your timeline and the demands of your event/trip.

8) How do you balance strength, skill, and conditioning?

It depends on your goals, current capacity, and what your sport needs most right now. Generally we use training cycles (periodisation) to prioritise the right qualities at the right time, while keeping everything else ticking over. The goal is progress without burnout — strong enough to perform, fit enough to last, resilient enough to repeat it week after week.

9) How long until I see results?

Most people feel early wins within 2–4 weeks (better structure, better recovery, more confidence and consistency). Meaningful strength and capacity changes usually show up over 6–12 weeks, and the durability compound effect happens over months. We aim for progress you can actually sustain.

10) Pricing + payment options

Program Only: $25/week
Coaching: $55/week

Payment is billed weekly. If you ever need to change tiers, pause, or adjust support level, message us — we’ll keep it simple.

11) Can I cancel or pause?

Yes. You can pause your account for up to 6 weeks if you need time for holidays, recovery, or life admin. If you stop entirely, you’re always welcome back — no sign-up fees when you return.

12) What if my schedule is chaotic?

That’s exactly why your program is built to be flexible. We can structure minimum effective dose sessions, optional add-ons, and clear priorities so you always know what matters most that week. Consistency beats perfection.
